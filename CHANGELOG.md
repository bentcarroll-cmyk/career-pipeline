# Changelog

## 0.1.9 — 2026-09-15 (local release)

- Apply an optional, explicitly approved geographic intake policy before detailed fit review; retain full source inventory, exclusion proofs, and uncertain locations.
- Bind location aliases and policy to criteria approval; automatically reopen stale prefilter decisions when content, criteria, or matcher semantics change.
- Reject unsupported board query filters instead of silently implying provider-side filtering.
- Keep independent posting IDs separate even when their internal requisition matches; provide hash-guarded legacy queue recovery with complete archived history.
- Flush final HTML-parser text and replay older normalizations additively without replacing native responses, intake receipts, valid resolutions, or newer descriptions.
- Preserve all existing resume-quality behavior and private application state.
- Retain compound country-only locations for review and reopen prior automatic exclusions under the updated matcher.
- Preserve newer posting content and decisions during interrupted page or pointer replay, including equal-time recovery.
- Repair legacy posting URL round-trips with versioned observations while retaining original snapshots and history.
- Resume legacy packets through an explicit final-PDF revalidation receipt without replacing their original editorial approvals.
- Recognize conventional resume section headings and wrapped target titles without treating legitimate work history as a headline.

## 0.1.8 — 2026-09-15 (local release)

- Track declared discovery queries, raw responses, missing-description pointers, and unfinished retrieval separately from job reviews.
- Require provider exhaustion and reconciled capture/intake evidence before source checkpoints advance; legacy review-only completion is not discovery completeness.
- Add complete-board Greenhouse/Ashby collection and resumable Lever pagination with fixed HTTPS hosts and no credentials.
- Preserve capped Indeed and unstructured web responses as explicitly supplementary scopes, including all extracted listings and pending pointers.
- Replay interrupted captures before requesting more results and retain failed attempts, invalid responses, and missing enabled-source work.
- Require the known search plan to be registered before requests; document safer filtering, smaller review batches, and distinct source-record/opportunity counts.

## 0.1.7 — 2026-09-15 (local release)

- Measure final resume PDFs before quality approval and local delivery; reject empty or underfilled pages, large internal gaps, unreadable body text, stale layout evidence, and incorrect page counts.
- Require supported evidence expansion and actual reflow before declaring a two-page resume complete; generic scanability explanations cannot override the layout check.
- Treat explicit user confirmations as career evidence, retain supported accomplishments when measurement details remain uncertain, and ask targeted questions only after reviewing the canonical sources.
- Add the optional `pdf` dependency group for deterministic PDF checks; the core discovery and storage helpers remain dependency-free.
