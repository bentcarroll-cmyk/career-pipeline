## Resume defaults

- Target length: 2 substantively full, readable pages
- Structure: clean, text-focused, and ATS-friendly
- Evidence style: specific, factual, and contribution-aware
- Source priority: later explicit user corrections, canonical career interview notes, reconciliation decisions, preserved primary resume, then historical tailored resumes
- User-confirmed facts and canonical interview answers need no independent third-party verification; retain supported accomplishments when only a measurement is uncertain
- Read full relevant source evidence and record selected and omitted candidates with specific reasons before treating the record as exhausted
- Draft for substance, render before space-related cuts, and use relevant additions plus natural reflow to fill available capacity; no arbitrary word or bullet ceilings
- Maximum unused writable bottom height or internal body-text gap per page: 38.1 points, measured from the final PDF; no generic reviewer waiver
- Preserve approved readable type, normal margins, and ordinary paragraph spacing; do not disguise underfill through formatting or filler
- Ask precise unresolved career questions only after source review and supported expansion trials; do not silently deliver a sparse resume as complete

## Cover-letter defaults

- Enabled: true
- Target length: 1 page
- Style: direct, role-specific, and evidence-led
