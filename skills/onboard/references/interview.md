# Career and ambitions interview

Ask in manageable conversational groups and persist progress. Cover:

1. Accomplishments and measurable outcomes.
2. The user's own contribution versus team or company results.
3. Scope: people, budget, systems, geography, complexity, and decision authority.
4. Career transitions and the chronology needed to explain them accurately.
5. Strengths, distinctive working patterns, and evidence supporting each claim.
6. Ambitions, target work, role families, industries, company stages, and leadership scope.
7. Compensation expectations and acceptable uncertainty.
8. Geography, workplace model, relocation, travel, and authorization constraints.
9. Timing, deadlines, deal-breakers, and other practical constraints.
10. Claims that remain uncertain or need corroboration.

Use stable evidence identifiers in the career profile. Mark unsupported or uncertain claims; never turn an inference into fact. A later explicit correction overrides conflicting resume extraction.
