# Evidence-led tailoring

## Retrieve the career record before selecting evidence

Read the approved profile and its source inventory, then the full relevant canonical interview notes, reconciliation decisions, and current corrections. Include current work beyond stories that share keywords with the posting. Use prior resumes for framing and candidate discovery; verify their claims against the authoritative career record. A compressed profile and repeated historical wording do not establish that all useful evidence has been considered.

Apply this source priority: later explicit user corrections, canonical career interview notes, reconciliation decisions, preserved primary resume, then historical tailored resumes. Record the actual workspace-relative source paths, SHA-256 hashes, and passages or evidence IDs consulted under `working/`. The posting establishes employer needs and employer facts; it cannot substantiate the candidate's history. Preserve the approved-profile boundary and record any newly confirmed evidence through the workspace's profile-update workflow before relying on a changed profile hash.

The user's explicit confirmations and canonical interview answers are valid evidence without independent third-party proof. Judge support at the claim level: an uncertain number restricts that number, not the supported action, leadership responsibility, scope, decision, or qualitative result. Preserve measured, reported, modeled, forecast, and in-progress distinctions and the user's versus the team's contribution. For example, user-confirmed team alignment may be included without inventing a resulting savings percentage. Never invent a metric, responsibility, technology, credential, date, or outcome.

## Build the hiring argument and evidence reserve

Derive three to five central hiring outcomes from the full posting's responsibilities and record the supporting passages. Distinguish threshold qualifications from differentiating accomplishments. Assess candidates by relevance, contribution, business significance, scope, distinctiveness, recency, and factual support. A supported leadership outcome can be stronger than a quantified routine task.

Keep a concise private content brief with a selected-evidence list and reserve. Each candidate entry records:

- the source path and passage or evidence ID;
- the supported contribution, result or capability, scope, and necessary qualifiers;
- the hiring outcome it supports and what distinct conclusion it adds;
- whether selected, reserved for expansion, or omitted, and a specific reason;
- for an omission due to space, the rendered trial showing actual overflow or displacement of stronger proof.

Include meaningful context compressed out of selected stories: decisions, alignment, adoption mechanisms, execution, scope, and supported results. Distinguish low relevance, duplication, an exact unsupported claim, and demonstrated space limits. “Other claims unverified,” “already two pages,” and an assumed bullet budget are not sufficient omission reasons. Check the original source before rejecting a claim as unsupported; record exactly what remains unknown and what supported wording can still be used.

Draft the concise, complete hiring case before cuts for length. Current work should establish the candidate's present level; older work earns detail when it supplies unusually direct or otherwise missing proof. Preserve honest reverse chronology and official titles and dates. There are no arbitrary word, bullet, role-block, or recent-work percentage ceilings. Weak or repetitive material may be removed editorially; space-related cuts require an actual rendered fit trial.

## Review selection and prominence

When an independent reviewer is available, have them derive the hiring outcomes and strongest proof from the posting and career sources before reading the author's selection rationale. Otherwise perform a separate pass and record that limitation. Compare the weakest included accomplishment with the strongest useful omission or expansion. Also test whether both fit: useful evidence does not need to displace a stronger item when room remains.

The opening and first page should establish current level and the strongest reasons to interview. Compare up to two relevant prior resumes when available to check stable facts and meaningful differences in selection, order, and depth. Reuse accurate wording when it remains best; superficial novelty is unnecessary. After every expansion or reflow, review the complete hiring case again so added volume does not dilute relevance. Record exact selected/omitted decisions against the final PDF pages, source hashes, and final PDF hash.

The resume must be clean, text-focused, ATS-friendly, and exactly two pages by default. The cover letter must be role-specific, evidence-led, and exactly one page when enabled. Apply Workday or another ATS-specific structural rule only after verifying that system is the actual application destination.

Use a capability-based resume headline grounded in the approved profile. Never place the employer's exact target job title or the target employer in the resume identity block, and never present an unlabeled target title as if it were a role the user already holds. The target title belongs in packet metadata, filenames, the cover-letter subject, and application fields—not as the candidate's resume identity.

Do not include a requested, preferred, desired, or negotiable start date in the resume. Treat availability as recruiter-screening or application-form information and include it only when the user explicitly chooses to provide it in that channel.
